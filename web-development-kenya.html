What is the difference between web design and web development?

Design covers layout, colour, typography and the visitor's journey. Development builds the working site: pages, forms, databases, logins and integrations. Most projects need both, and we deliver them together.

Can you build a portal with logins?

Yes. We built a parent and learner portal for a Likoni school and can create logins, dashboards and records for schools, clinics and other organisations. See our school portal system.

Do you only build with WordPress?

No. We choose the approach that fits the project. Some sites suit a lightweight custom build, others suit a content system like WordPress. See our WordPress developer page for that route.

How long does web development take?

A typical business website takes one to three weeks once we have your content. Portals, stores and custom platforms take longer, and we agree the timeline in writing first.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We handle updates, content changes, fixes and improvements through website maintenance.
