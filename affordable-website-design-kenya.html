Affordable website design Kenya

Affordable should mean good value, not corners cut

Searching for an affordable web designer in Kenya usually leads to two extremes: very cheap templates that look like everyone else's, or agency quotes that feel out of reach. Neither helps a small business. What you need is a website that is priced sensibly and still builds trust.

We keep costs fair by being specific. We agree exactly which pages and features you need, reuse proven components instead of reinventing them, and plan in phases: launch a solid core first, then add a shop, a portal or extra pages when the budget allows. Our quote form even lets you choose a budget range, from under KES 30,000 upwards, so we can recommend something realistic.

Affordable does not mean basic on the things that matter. Every site is responsive, fast, and set up to be found on Google. Ask on WhatsApp
How much does an affordable website cost in Kenya?

It depends on pages and features. A simple business website costs far less than a portal or online store. Tell us your budget range and requirements on WhatsApp and we will recommend a realistic option with a clear quotation.

Is a cheaper website worse?

Not if it is scoped well. We keep cost down by focusing on essentials and reusing proven components, not by skipping mobile design, speed or basic SEO.

Can I pay in stages or add features later?

Yes. Many clients launch a core website first and add a shop, portal or more pages later. We plan the site so those additions fit neatly.

Do I pay for hosting and a domain too?

Domains and hosting are separate yearly costs paid to providers. We advise on and set them up, so you choose something sensible rather than overpaying.

When do I pay?

We agree the scope and price first, and you do not pay until you have agreed it. We confirm the payment arrangement in writing.
