FAQ

Questions clients often ask.

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, so the price depends on the type of website, the number of pages and any special features. Tell us what you need and we'll recommend a solution and share a clear quotation.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the size and complexity of the project. After we discuss your goals and content, we'll agree a realistic timeline before work begins.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can turn an outdated website into a cleaner, faster and more professional experience for today's customers.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, logo, photos and any content you already have. If something is missing, tell us and we'll advise on what will work best.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first, so it looks and works well on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support, including updates, content changes and improvements.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

We are Kenya-based and remote projects are welcome.
