FAQ

Questions clients often ask.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a website cost in Kenya?

Price depends on the type of website, the number of pages and any special features such as a portal, online payments or a shop. Send us your requirements and we will recommend a solution with a clear quotation.

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical business website takes one to three weeks once we have your content. Larger projects such as school portals or online stores take longer. We agree a realistic timeline before work begins.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We turn outdated websites into cleaner, faster and more professional experiences, and we keep your existing domain.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, logo, photos and any content you already have. If something is missing, we advise on what will work best.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first, so it works well on phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Do you handle domains and hosting?

Yes. We can advise on and set up your domain name, hosting and business email so you do not have to deal with the technical side.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance and support, including updates, content changes and improvements.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. We are Kenya-based and remote projects are welcome.
