E-commerce website development

An online store built around how Kenyans actually shop

Kenyans already shop through Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp chats, but those channels make it hard to show a full catalogue, keep prices consistent or build a lasting brand. An e-commerce website puts every product, price and order step in one place that you control and customers can trust.

Our e-commerce website development covers the full journey: product catalogue and categories, product pages with clear photos and descriptions, a simple cart or order button, and an admin area where you add products and handle orders. We design it mobile-first because most shoppers will arrive on a phone. Payment options can be planned into the scope as your store matures.

You do not have to launch everything at once. Many sellers start with a catalogue and order by WhatsApp, then add online checkout. If you are mainly after a customer-facing shop design, see our online shopping website design page. For a national overview of store services, visit our e-commerce website development Kenya page, and for the engineering side see web development services.

Ask on WhatsApp
How much does an e-commerce website cost in Kenya?

Cost depends on the number of products, features such as checkout or accounts, and how much customisation you want. Share your needs on WhatsApp and we will recommend an option with a clear quotation.

Can customers order through WhatsApp instead of paying online?

Yes. Many Kenyan stores start this way. Customers choose a product and the site opens WhatsApp with the item filled in, and you confirm payment and delivery in the chat.

Can I add products myself?

Yes. We build an admin area for adding products, changing prices and managing orders, and we train you at handover.

Is an e-commerce website the same as an online shopping website?

They overlap. This page focuses on development and store management, while our online shopping website design page focuses on the customer's shopping experience.

Can you redesign my existing online store?

Yes. We can modernise a slow or outdated store through website redesign and keep your domain.
